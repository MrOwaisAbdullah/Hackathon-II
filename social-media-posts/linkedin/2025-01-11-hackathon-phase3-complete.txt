Hackathon II — Phase 3 is done.

The goal was to build an AI agent.
Not a chatbot that just talks.
An agent that actually DOES things.

Frontend on Vercel, backend on HuggingFace Spaces.

Simple enough, right?

Not quite.

Here's what hit me:

Deployment failed 15+ times.
ESLint errors blocked the build.
TypeScript broke on ref types.
Missing UI components appeared out of nowhere.

The biggest blocker?

ChatKit domain verification.

It worked perfectly on localhost.
But production threw IntegrationError immediately.

I spent hours trying to suppress the error.
Client-side handlers didn't work.
Global error listeners didn't catch it.

Turns out, ChatKit's CDN enforces domain verification.
Even with a self-hosted backend.

The solution wasn't code.
It was registering the domain at OpenAI dashboard.
Using NEXT_PUBLIC_CHATKIT_DOMAIN_KEY in Vercel.

Once that was fixed, everything clicked.

What I shipped:

An AI task assistant that can:
👉 Create tasks and projects
👉 Update assignments
👉 Delete completed work
👉 Manage team workflows
👉 Track time entries
👉 Filter by status or assignee

It's a personal agent.
You chat with it, it takes action.

Plus:
👉 Voice input integration
👉 Dark/light theme support
👉 Fullscreen toggle mode
👉 Marketing pages (pricing, privacy, terms)

Main lesson from Phase 3:

Local development hides real-world problems.
Production deployment exposes them fast.

Domain verification, CORS, environment variables.
These don't show up until you ship.

The best way to learn?
Deploy early. Break things. Fix them.

Phase 4 starts next week.
