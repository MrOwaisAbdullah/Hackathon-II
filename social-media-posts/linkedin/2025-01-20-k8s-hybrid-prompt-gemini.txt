## 🚀 How to Use with Gemini

### Step 1: Access Gemini
- Go to: https://gemini.google.com
- Sign in with your Google account

### Step 2: Enter the Prompt
1. Click the image icon (or type "generate image")
2. Copy and paste the **Master Prompt** above
3. Click Generate

### Step 3: Refine if Needed
If first attempt isn't perfect, add specific feedback:
- "Make the journey path more prominent"
- "Add more glow effects to the icons"
- "Make the architecture diagram boxes larger"
- "Use darker navy background"

### Step 4: Download and Upload to LinkedIn
- Download the generated image (PNG)
- Upload to LinkedIn with your post

## 💡 Pro Tips for Gemini Image Generation

1. **Text Rendering**: Gemini 2.5 Pro is excellent at rendering readable text
2. **Style Precision**: Use brand names like "Linear" or "Vercel" for consistent aesthetic
3. **Iterate**: Generate 2-3 versions and pick the best
4. **Aspect Ratio**: Always specify 16:9 for LinkedIn posts
5. **Color Specificity**: Use hex codes for consistent branding
