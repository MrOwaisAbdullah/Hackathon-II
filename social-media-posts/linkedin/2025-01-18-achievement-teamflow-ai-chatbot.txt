Here's the setup:

Primary model: OpenRouter with Gemini 2.0 Flash (free tier)
Fallback model: OpenAI API with GPT-5 Nano

When OpenRouter hits rate limits (429 errors)?
System automatically retries with OpenAI.
Transparent to the user.
Zero downtime.

The architecture keeps it simple:
One FastAPI process.
One port to expose.
MCP server mounted at /mcp endpoint.
No separate services to manage.

Biggest lesson from this build:
OpenAI's hosted ChatKit is great for demos.
But production apps need custom backends.

You need to:
 • Wrap your own models (Gemini, OpenRouter, etc.)
 • Handle rate limits gracefully with fallback
 • Persist chat history yourself
 • Integrate with your existing services

The OpenAI Agents SDK makes this possible.
MCPServerStreamableHttp connects everything.
