# Eco Modern

A high-contrast, modern theme featuring clean black and white aesthetics with vibrant lime green accents, inspired by eco-friendly technology brands.

## Color Palette

- **Lime Green**: `#a3e635` - Vibrant brand primary and accent color
- **Deep Black**: `#18181b` - Primary action buttons and high-contrast elements
- **Clean White**: `#ffffff` - Primary background and negative space
- **Zinc Gray**: `#f4f4f5` - Muted backgrounds and secondary UI elements

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Sans Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Modern SaaS dashboards, eco-tech startups, high-contrast interfaces, sustainability reporting, and clean corporate presentations.
